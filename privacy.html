    What we collect when you use this website, and what we do with it.

    This is placeholder privacy copy prepared for the prototype. It must be reviewed and replaced by a qualified lawyer, and reconciled with the analytics, forms and third-party services actually in use, before the site is published.

    1. What we collect

    If you contact us we hold the details you provide — typically a name, an email address, a company and a description of your enquiry. We do not collect special category data and we do not ask for it.

    2. How we use it

    We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, to keep a record of our business correspondence, and to meet our legal record-keeping obligations. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

    3. Cookies

    This site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any essential storage used for interface preferences is described here when it is added.

    4. Third-party services

    Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving the font files. If you are in the EEA, consider self-hosting the font to remove that third-party request.

    5. Retention

    Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to support the business relationship and to meet statutory retention requirements, after which they are deleted.

    6. Your rights

    Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to how it is used. Contact us and we will action any such request within the statutory period.
